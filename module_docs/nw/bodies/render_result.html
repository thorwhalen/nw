nw.bodies.render_result

Body schema for render results — the output of a render Transform.

URI: annot://schema/render-result/v1

A render-result records that a shot was rendered: which strategy ran, where the output landed, the video lacing.Artifact it produced, and the estimated cost. Its provenance.was_derived_from includes the shot annotation’s id, so a freshness traversal from the shot finds the render.

This is the output_kind of the render-strategy Transforms (see nw.transforms._adapters.render_strategy). It is intentionally small — the heavy data (the actual mp4) is the referenced Artifact, not the body.
